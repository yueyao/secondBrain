# Design Context

## Users

Single owner (dynamic) of a personal knowledge workbench. Context: daily review of imported articles, curating claims/nodes, composing and publishing articles, and checking system health. Sessions are long, desktop-first, data-dense; mobile is secondary (quick checks).

## Brand Personality

Precise, instrument-like, quietly technical. The interface should feel like a well-maintained engineering station: measured, legible, trustworthy; not playful, not decorative, not "AI demo".

## Aesthetic Direction

Light engineering blueprint.

- Palette: cool blue-grey paper, blue-tinted ink, **signal orange** as the single accent, muted blueprint blue for links/data, restrained green/amber/red only for status.
- Surfaces: fine engineering grid on the page background; panels are flat, sharp-cornered, 1px ruled; small registration/corner marks are the signature detail.
- Typography: condensed/DIN-like display for headings and numerals; PingFang-class sans for Chinese body; monospace only for data, paths, timestamps and metrics. No all-caps eyebrow labels, no gradient text, no glowing accents.
- Density: dense but rhythmic; tables are ledger-like with tabular numerals; section separation by rules, not by stacking rounded cards.
- Motion: one restrained page-load reveal (opacity/translate), reduced-motion respected; no hover animation on every card.

## Anti-references

- Dark mode + cyan/neon accent, purple-blue gradients, glassmorphism.
- Identical rounded cards with generic shadows, pill badges everywhere.
- Sparse "minimal" pages with gray text and no hierarchy.

## Design Principles

1. Structure is information: grids, rules, ticks and readouts should encode hierarchy, not decorate.
2. One accent: signal orange marks state, focus, and the current position; everything else is ink/blue-grey.
3. Data first: numbers, counts and timestamps use tabular/mono treatment and always align.
4. Long lists are paginated or folded; no unbounded page grows.
5. Every screen answers "where am I / what changed / what can I do next" within one glance.
